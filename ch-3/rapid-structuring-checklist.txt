ASSET 6 — RAPID-STRUCTURING CHECKLIST
The AI Leverage Playbook — Chapter 3
=====================================================================

HOW TO USE THIS FILE

Structuring is a sequence of passes, each with its own input and
output, done in separate sittings. Merging passes is how people end
up with a manuscript they cannot reorder without rewriting it.

One page, five passes, the exact question to ask at each, and the
three cuts to run in pass four. Work top to bottom. Do not run two
passes in one sitting.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PASS 1 — PROMISE AND PROOF
---------------------------------------------------------------------

INPUT:        Finished drafts plus the one-sentence promise
TRANSFORMATION: Ask of each draft what it proves about the promise;
              cut what proves nothing
OUTPUT:       Chapter list where every entry carries a
              promise-relevance line

THE QUESTION AT THIS PASS:
  "What does this chapter prove about the promise?"

  [ ] Every chapter has a written answer to that question
  [ ] Every chapter with no answer is deleted, not moved, not
      "saved for a bonus PDF"
  [ ] Good drafts were deleted anyway. Quality is not the test here —
      relevance is

NOTE: This is the pass that removes work. A book is defined by its
exclusions more than its inclusions, which is the opposite of how
generated material behaves by default.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
PASS 2 — SKELETON
---------------------------------------------------------------------

INPUT:        Chapter list
TRANSFORMATION: Order chapters as the steps a reader takes; budget
              pages per step
OUTPUT:       Ordered outline with page budgets

THE QUESTION AT THIS PASS:
  "What has the reader produced by the end of this chapter?"

  [ ] Every chapter has a concrete answer — a file, a table, a
      sentence, a listing, a price
  [ ] Any chapter whose answer is "understanding" is moved or cut
  [ ] The order is the reader's action sequence, not the order you
      briefed the material in
  [ ] Every chapter carries a page budget

NOTE: The test is mechanical. If the answer is "understanding," the
chapter is in the wrong place or should not exist.

Generated order is your learning sequence. A reader does not need
your learning sequence. They need their action sequence.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
PASS 3 — EVIDENCE MAPPING
---------------------------------------------------------------------

INPUT:        Skeleton plus your sourced Ideas rows
TRANSFORMATION: Attach a source to every claim and number; flag gaps
OUTPUT:       Mapped outline with gaps visible

THE QUESTION AT THIS PASS:
  "Where is the checkable source for this claim?"

  [ ] Every number traces to an Ideas row with a Source
  [ ] Every named framework traces to a source or to your own
      definition, written down
  [ ] Every gap is flagged visibly, not smoothed over
  [ ] Nothing was given a source by searching for one that agrees
      with the claim

NOTE: This is the only pass you should not compress. Ungrounded
claims survive pass four easily, because compression makes a wrong
sentence shorter and more confident, not more true.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
PASS 4 — ASSEMBLY AND COMPRESSION
---------------------------------------------------------------------

INPUT:        Ordered skeleton plus drafts
TRANSFORMATION: Paste into order, cut the three dead shapes, rewrite
              seams
OUTPUT:       Full manuscript at 70 to 80 percent of draft length

THE QUESTION AT THIS PASS:
  "Is this sentence information, or is it restatement?"

THE THREE COMPRESSION CUTS — run all three:

  CUT 1 — THE HEADING RESTATEMENT
  A sentence that restates the heading. The heading already said it.
  A reader scanning has already received it twice.

    [ ] Every heading was read, and the sentence after it was
        checked for restatement

  CUT 2 — THE PARAPHRASE PARAGRAPH
  A paragraph that repeats the paragraph above it in different
  words. Generated prose does this constantly, because the pattern
  that produces fluent text also produces paraphrase as a form of
  emphasis.

    [ ] Every paragraph was checked against the one above it

  CUT 3 — THE FILLER TRANSITION
  A transition that exists only to fill space: the one that
  announces what the next section will cover, or recalls what the
  previous one covered. In a 40-page product, a reader can hold the
  structure without being reminded.

    [ ] Every "in the next section," "as we discussed," and "now
        that we have covered" was cut

  [ ] Final length is between 70 and 80 percent of draft length.
      If it is at 95 percent, you have edited, not compressed.

NOTE: This is not tightening and not rewriting. Three shapes, found
and deleted. It is not a better writer doing this — it is a stricter
reader.

Set your target in pages the reader must finish, not words you must
produce. Then hit it by cutting. A word-count target is an
instruction to pad, and the engine will fill it, competently, with
restatement.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
PASS 5 — PROFESSIONAL PACKAGING
---------------------------------------------------------------------

INPUT:        Manuscript
TRANSFORMATION: Cover, title and subtitle, interior formatting,
              front and back matter
OUTPUT:       Upload-ready file

THE QUESTION AT THIS PASS:
  "Would a stranger recognise this from a thumbnail?"

  [ ] Cover is legible at thumbnail width — title readable before
      the subtitle is legible
  [ ] Cover carries one idea, not three
  [ ] Title names the outcome
  [ ] Subtitle names the audience, the timeframe, and the obstacle
  [ ] Interior formatting is consistent: same heading style at every
      level, same paragraph spacing, same table treatment
  [ ] No decorative elements that behave unpredictably in a
      reflowable file
  [ ] Final page is one action, in the imperative, completable in
      under twenty minutes
  [ ] The final page carries exactly one ask — one link, one email
      capture, or one review request

NOTE: A template plus discipline beats a designer, because a
designer optimizes one cover and a template optimizes a catalog. The
reader who buys product two already recognises product one from the
thumbnail.

A page with three asks converts worse than a page with one, because
it converts the reader into a decision-maker at the exact moment
they have the least patience for it.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIVE PASSES AT A GLANCE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  PASS  INPUT                    →  OUTPUT
  1     Drafts + promise         →  Chapter list with promise-relevance
  2     Chapter list             →  Ordered outline with page budgets
  3     Skeleton + sourced rows  →  Mapped outline with gaps visible
  4     Skeleton + drafts        →  Manuscript at 70–80% of draft
  5     Manuscript               →  Upload-ready file

Two rules that keep the passes separate:

  - One pass per sitting. Merging passes is how you get a manuscript
    you cannot reorder without rewriting.
  - Pass 3 is never compressed and never skipped.
